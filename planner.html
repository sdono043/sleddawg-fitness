Greensprings 24 · Race Strategy Loop course
🔄
Loop Psychology
2.215 mi loops means you'll see your gear every ~25 min. Use this — restock food/water every 2-3 loops. Set mini-goals of 5 loops at a time, not 100 miles.
🌙
Night Running
Race starts 8am, so miles 50-70 hit around 2-4am. Train night runs in weeks 14-16. Your training plan includes them. Headlamp required.
⚡
Pacing for 100
2.5 mph average = 24 min per loop. That's a slow jog/fast walk. Start at 3.5 mph and walk to 2 mph by mile 70+. Run/walk from mile 1 — don't wait until you're forced to.
🍬
Nutrition (per the plan)
220-240 cal/hour. ~4 miles per hour = fuel every loop. Test your exact race-day food in training. Skittles + soft granola bars proven for this course.
🏕️
Base Camp Setup
Loop check-in is 50 yards from parking. Set up a tent with: drop bags per loop count, chair, multiple shoe changes, dry socks every 4-6 hours, headlamp + backup.
🦵
Flat Course Training
No elevation required, but repetitive flat can destroy quads differently than trails. Add road miles late in training. Practice your exact run/walk intervals on flat ground.
